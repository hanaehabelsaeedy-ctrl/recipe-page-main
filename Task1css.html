Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic ormelette combines beaten eggs Cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

  • Preparation: 5 minutes

  3. Cook the omelette: Once the butter is melted and bubbling, pour in the eggs. Tilt the pan to ensure the eggsevenly coat the surface.

  4. Add fillings (optional): When the eggs begin to set at the edges but are still slightly runny in the middle, sprinkle your chosen Allings over ane half of the omelette.

  5. Fold and serve: As the omelette continues to cook, carefully lift one edge and fold it over the fillings. Let it cook for another minute, then slide it onto a plate.

  6. Enjoy: Serve hot, with additional salt and pepper if needed.
